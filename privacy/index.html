Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 6, 2026 • Application: FEDU Video Manager (video quan ly)

1. Overview & Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how FEDU ("we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, and safeguards information when you connect and use the FEDU Video Manager (registered as video quan ly) via TikTok Developer APIs and our website (https://fedu.vn).

We respect your privacy and are committed to complying with TikTok Developer Terms of Service, GDPR, and relevant data protection regulations.

2. Information We Collect

When you authenticate and authorize our application through TikTok OAuth Login Kit and Content Posting API, we may receive:

  • Basic Profile Data: Your TikTok open_id, union_id, display name, and avatar URL to identify authorized channel sessions.
  • Video & Content Metadata: Video titles, descriptions, hashtags, publish timestamps, and upload status required for publishing and scheduling.
  • Analytics Data: View counts, likes, and comment metrics retrieved via authorized APIs for performance reporting.

We DO NOT collect, store, or have access to your personal TikTok account passwords, payment credentials, or private chat messages.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the data collected strictly for the following functional purposes:

  • To authenticate and connect your authorized TikTok account.
  • To upload, schedule, and publish video content directly to your TikTok channel upon your explicit request.
  • To display performance dashboards and upload status reports.
  • To troubleshoot system errors and ensure API uptime.

We will NEVER sell, lease, or monetize your data with third parties or ad brokers.

4. Data Storage and Security

Your access tokens and refresh tokens are encrypted and securely stored with strict access controls (restricted file permissions and TLS encryption). We do not keep video files on temporary servers once they have been successfully dispatched and confirmed by the TikTok API endpoint.

5. Data Retention & Deletion Instructions

You have full control over your data at any time:

  • Revoke Access: You can revoke the application's access at any moment via your TikTok app settings (Settings > Security > Manage App Permissions).
  • Request Data Deletion: If you wish to delete all stored tokens and metadata associated with your account, please send an email to vietndj@gmail.com with the subject line "TikTok Data Deletion Request". We will permanently delete all records within 48 business hours.
